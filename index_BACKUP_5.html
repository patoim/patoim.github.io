The story
Built out of frustration,
not inspiration

As a student at the University of Texas at El Paso, I was constantly juggling labs, exams, readings, and deadlines across multiple classes every single semester. I tried everything — sticky notes, phone reminders, random apps, even just hoping I'd remember.

Nothing worked the way my brain needed it to. Everything felt either too complicated, too basic, or simply not built for students like me. So instead of settling, I built the tool I always wished existed.

"I got tired of missing deadlines and losing track of everything. I couldn't find the right tool — so I built one."
